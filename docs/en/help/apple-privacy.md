# YouTweak Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 24, 2026**

[English](../../en/help/apple-privacy.md) | [简体中文](../../zh-cn/help/apple-privacy.md) | [日本語](../../ja/help/apple-privacy.md)

This Privacy Policy applies to the YouTweak application and its bundled Safari extension on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS (collectively, "YouTweak").

## Privacy Summary

The developer of YouTweak does not collect, receive, store, sell, or rent your personal data or use it for the developer's own purposes. YouTweak does not require an account, contain advertising, analytics, trackers, telemetry, or background diagnostic reporting, or operate servers or databases that receive user data. Third-party network processing required for particular features is described separately below.

## Data We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect or retain any personal data or usage data, including:

- names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or other contact information;
- account credentials, payment information, or purchase history;
- precise or approximate location;
- contacts, photos, audio, camera, or microphone data;
- device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or tracking identifiers;
- browsing history, search history, YouTube watch history, or page content;
- usage, performance, crash, or other diagnostic data.

## On-Device Processing and Local Storage

After you explicitly grant Safari website access, the YouTweak extension runs on `*.youtube.com`. To provide currently enabled features, the extension may read and modify the current YouTube page's content and URL, as well as related network requests and responses. This processing takes place in your browser. The developer does not receive this content or save it on any developer-controlled server.

Your extension settings, feature preferences, caches, and version-notice state, including playback-speed or subtitle preferences saved for individual YouTube channels, are stored in local or synchronized storage provided by Safari or WebExtension. If you enable browser or system synchronization, the platform provider may synchronize these settings under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer cannot access that synchronized data.

## Network Connections and Third-Party Services

YouTweak does not send data to the developer, but the following features cause your device to connect directly to the third-party services that provide the relevant functionality:

- **YouTube and Google services:** YouTweak operates on the YouTube website and may make requests to YouTube that are necessary for selected features. When translation features are enabled (some may be enabled by default and can be disabled in settings), YouTube page text, comments, or subtitles selected for translation may be sent directly to the Google translation service to return a translation. The developer does not receive or retain this content.
- **GitHub:** When you open release notes, help, source code, or issue-reporting pages, the application may retrieve public content from GitHub or open a GitHub page in your browser. The request does not include your YouTweak settings or YouTube page content.

Third-party services may receive information necessary for a normal network connection, such as an IP address and standard request information, and process it under their respective policies. Please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Apple may also separately process App Store, operating-system, synchronization, or diagnostic information under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); that information is not collected by YouTweak. YouTweak does not add identifiers for advertising or cross-app tracking and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Google, GitHub, and Apple provide independent services and do not operate a data-collection service on behalf of the YouTweak developer. The developer does not disclose personal data to advertising, analytics, or other third-party partners. If YouTweak ever engages a third party to process personal data on the developer's behalf, the developer will require that party to provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy, disclose the arrangement, and update this policy before processing begins.

## How Permissions Are Used

YouTweak requests only the permissions needed for its core functionality:

- **YouTube website access:** to read and modify YouTube pages, after you grant access, for the interface and playback features you enable;
- **Storage:** to save settings, preferences, and extension state in browser-provided storage;
- **Tabs:** to open help or project links and, when necessary, update tabs related to extension functionality.

These permissions are not used to build a browsing profile, track websites you visit outside YouTube, or send any data to the developer. You can revoke website access or disable YouTweak at any time in Safari's extension settings.

## Data Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

Because the developer does not collect or store your data, the developer has no user data to retain, export, or delete.

YouTweak's reset feature restores feature configuration to its defaults and overwrites saved configuration, but it does not delete all extension data. To remove other local settings and caches, use Safari's website or extension data controls and uninstall YouTweak; settings synchronized by the platform can be managed through the applicable browser or system synchronization settings. You can also disable optional features such as translation to stop future translation requests or revoke YouTube website access in Safari's extension settings.

Google, GitHub, and Apple apply their own retention and deletion rules to information they receive directly. The developer cannot access or delete that information on your behalf. You can use the privacy controls or contact methods described in the third-party policies linked above to ask the relevant service to access, manage, or delete information it holds.

## Children's Privacy

YouTweak is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

## Security

YouTweak reduces privacy risk by operating no data-collection servers, prioritizing on-device processing, using system- and browser-provided sandboxing and storage mechanisms, and limiting permissions to those needed for core functionality.

## Changes to This Policy

If YouTweak's data practices change, the developer will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before the new practices take effect and will request consent when required by applicable law or App Store rules. The latest version will always be published on this page with a revised effective date.
